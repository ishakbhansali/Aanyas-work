AANYA ART GALLERY UPDATE

This is an update to your existing website, not a standalone website.
1. Unzip this package.
2. Upload its contents to the SAME folder as index.html in your existing GitHub repository.
3. Replace matching website files and merge the assets folder, preserving existing assets.
4. Keep your existing comics, knowledge images, styles.css, language.css, app.js, content.json and PDF.
5. Open gallery.html through your website after the GitHub Pages update completes.
Do not upload the ZIP itself.

All 107 artworks are retained and grouped into seven sections, with proposed English and Swedish titles and descriptions. The photographs are rotated and gently brightened with a small contrast adjustment. There is no generative redrawing, cropping or perspective correction. Originals remain in your original photo archives.

The page loads 18 previews initially, with a load-more button; larger images load only when opened.
Gallery-Catalogue.csv lists all artwork IDs, titles, groups and rotations.
